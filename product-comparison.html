How to Choose the Right Tech

Make sense of the specifications and choose the hardware that best fits your daily workflow.

Processor (CPU)

Look at generation & cores. Creators benefit from multi-core chips (M4 Pro, Ryzen 9), while everyday tasks run perfectly on standard Core i7 chips.

Display Technology

OLED offers pure blacks. Choose high refresh rates (165Hz–360Hz) for ultra-smooth gaming; 60Hz is standard for normal office workloads.

Memory (RAM)

16GB is the sweet spot for multitasking. Go for 32GB or 64GB memory sizes for complex video editing, 3D rendering, or triple-A gaming.

Solid State Storage

Choose NVMe SSDs over mechanical HDDs. A 512GB SSD is enough for office files; heavy creative workloads require 1TB to 2TB storage.
